About Novak Djokovic

Novak Djokovic is a professional tennis player from Serbia. He is regarded as one of the greatest tennis players in history, known for his consistency, determination and remarkable achievements.

Quick Facts

  • Born: 22 May 1987
  • Birthplace: Belgrade, Serbia
  • Sport: Tennis
  • Plays: Right-handed

Career Highlights

  1. 24 Grand Slam singles titles
  2. Olympic singles gold medal 3
  3. Record 428 weeks as world No. 1
